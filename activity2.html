About our Restaurant

Established in 1995, this legendary multi-cuisine establishment is a cornerstone of the Malabar culinary scene. It is widely celebrated for its warm, family-friendly ambience and a massive menu that flawlessly blends authentic Malabary heritage dishes (like slow-cooked Kuttanadan beef pot curry and Malabar biriyani) with Arab, Thai, and Chinese delicacies. Famed for its signature items like Kunafa and Khataif chicken, it has evolved from a cozy local eatery into an award-winning brand revered for premium dine-in comfort and grand event catering.
